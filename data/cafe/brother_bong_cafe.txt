
Brother Bong Cafe is a beloved café and brunch spot in Siem Reap, known for its excellent coffee, fresh & healthy meals, and relaxed local vibe. Founded by a young Cambodian couple — with award-winning barista Bunthoeun behind the espresso machine — it’s become a favorite for both locals and travelers seeking a quality café experience beyond just drinks

Key Highlights:
- Specialty Coffee & Drinks: Offers a full range of coffee drinks including espresso, flat white, cappuccino, long macchiato, and smoothies. The coffee is frequently praised as some of the best in Siem Reap.
- Mad Monkey Hostels
- Brunch & All-Day Menu: Serves breakfast, brunch, and lunch with a mix of Western and Khmer dishes, from smoothie bowls and avocado toast to noodle soups and salads — vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are clearly marked.
- Tripadvisor
- +1
- Relaxed Locale: A laid-back, open-air café with outdoor seating and a welcoming atmosphere, great for casual meals or lingering coffee breaks.
- Mad Monkey Hostels
- Friendly Service: Known for attentive, well-trained staff and a warm, inviting vibe.
- Tripadvisor
- Eco-Friendly Touches: The café emphasizes eco-friendly practices like using bamboo straws and plant-based packaging for takeaway.
