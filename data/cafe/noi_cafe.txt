
Noi Café is a popular local coffee spot in Siem Reap known for its relaxed vibe, great-tasting coffee at affordable prices, and convenient location near the Old Market and riverside area. It’s a favorite with both travelers and locals who want a cozy place to enjoy a quality cup of coffee, work, or chill between exploring the city.

Key Highlights:
- Charming Local Café: A laid-back coffee shop with greenery around and open-air seating that feels refreshing and rustic.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Great Coffee Variety: Offers a range of coffee drinks — from hot Americano to iced lattes and cappuccinos — often made with Brazilian beans and praised for their flavor and value.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Affordable Prices: Coffee prices start very low (around $1–$2), making it one of the best values for specialty-style coffee in town.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Work-Friendly: Many visitors appreciate plug points, ceiling fans, and a layout suitable for laptops or relaxed conversations.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Friendly Service: Staff are known for being welcoming, and many regulars feel like the café quickly becomes a go-to spot.
