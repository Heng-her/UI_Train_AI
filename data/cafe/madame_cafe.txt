
Madame Café is a charming riverside café in Siem Reap that’s beloved for its relaxed garden setting, friendly service, and good coffee and local dishes — making it a lovely stop if you’re exploring the riverfront or nearby Old Market area

Key Highlights:
- Riverside Garden Vibe: Set by the Siem Reap River with trees, flowers, and a peaceful garden atmosphere, the café is perfect for chilling with a coffee or tea and watching life unfold by the water.
- Wanderlog
- Great Ambience & Staff: Visitors often highlight the cute, calm vibe and friendly staff, who sometimes serve iced tea while you wait — a lovely touch.
- Wanderlog
- Coffee & Beverages: Offers fresh coffee, iced tea, and other cool drinks ideal for escaping the heat after sightseeing.
- Wanderlog
- Affordable Local Fare: In addition to drinks, the café serves local Cambodian dishes like noodle soups, fried rice, and breakfast options at reasonable prices (local café range).
- Wanderlog
- Hidden Gem Feel: Known as a relaxed, less touristy spot where both locals and travelers mingle in a laid-back outdoor setting.

Visitor Information:
- Location: 4 Pokambor Ave, Krong Siem Reap, right by the riverfront and within walking distance of Old Market. https://maps.app.goo.gl/vpc3iuahya5z4WW4A
- Wanderlog
- Getting There: Walk, tuk-tuk, bicycle, or motorbike from central Siem Reap.
- Wanderlog
- Best Time to Visit: Morning to late afternoon — great for coffee, lunch, or a relaxed drink while watching river views.
- Wanderlog
- Entrance Fee: Free — pay only for orders.
- Facilities: Outdoor seating in garden/riverside areas, free Wi-Fi typical of cafés, and friendly staff service.

Tips:
- Visit earlier in the day to enjoy the riverside vibe before it gets busier later in the afternoon.
- Wanderlog
- Pair your coffee with a local dish — reviewers have noted good, simple Khmer meals alongside drinks.
- Wanderlog
- The café’s garden seating and décor have a cozy, rustic charm that visitors enjoy for photos and relaxing breaks.

Why Visit:
- To enjoy a peaceful riverside coffee break, ideal after exploring Old Market or Pub Street.
- Wanderlog
- To experience local café culture in a scenic garden setting.
- Wanderlog
- Great for casual meals and refreshing drinks without the hustle of busier tourist cafés.
