
BROWN Roastery | Siem Reap is a well-known premium coffee destination located along the Siem Reap River, not far from the Old Market area. Founded by BROWN Coffee & Bakery, a leading Cambodian coffee brand, this roastery was created to showcase high-quality Cambodian and international coffee beans in a modern, stylish setting. It combines specialty coffee culture with elegant architecture, making it popular with locals, digital nomads, and tourists alike. BROWN Roastery represents Siem Reap’s contemporary café lifestyle while supporting locally sourced Cambodian coffee.

Key Highlights:
- Specialty Coffee & Roastery: Focuses on expertly roasted coffee, including Cambodian-grown beans and international selections, brewed by trained baristas.
- Modern Industrial Design: Spacious interior with high ceilings, glass walls, and minimalist décor that feels calm and upscale.
- Riverside Location: Situated near the Siem Reap River, offering a relaxing atmosphere away from busy nightlife streets.
- Food & Bakery: Serves pastries, cakes, brunch items, sandwiches, and light meals suitable for breakfast or lunch.
- Work-Friendly Space: Popular for meetings, studying, and remote work with comfortable seating and a quiet ambiance.
- Local Brand Pride: Highlights Cambodian entrepreneurship and coffee culture on an international level.

Visitor Information:
- Location: Along the Siem Reap River, a short distance from Old Market and central town. https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ea8VMqGY4wt2EbGV9
- Getting There: Easily reached by walking, tuk-tuk, bicycle, or motorbike.
- Best Time to Visit: Morning to early afternoon for a calm experience; late afternoon for a relaxed coffee break.
- Opening Hours: Generally open daily from morning until evening (hours may vary slightly).
- Entrance Fee: Free (pay only for food and drinks).
- Facilities: Air-conditioned indoor seating, restrooms, free Wi-Fi, and comfortable tables.
- Accessibility: Easy access with wide spaces suitable for most visitors.

Tips:
- Try their signature coffee or Cambodian single-origin brews.
- Visit in the morning if you want a quieter space for work or reading.
- Prices are higher than local cafés, but quality and atmosphere match the cost.
- Great spot to cool down after walking around town.
- Respect the calm environment—many visitors come to work or relax.

Why Visit:
- Experience high-quality Cambodian coffee in a modern setting.
- Enjoy a peaceful break from busy tourist areas.
- Support a successful local Cambodian brand.
- Ideal for coffee lovers, remote workers, and casual meetings.
- See the modern, creative side of Siem Reap’s café culture.
