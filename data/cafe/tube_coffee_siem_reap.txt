
TUBE COFFEE Siem Reap is a popular local coffee shop and café chain in Siem Reap that’s part of Cambodia’s growing homegrown coffee brand, Tube Coffee. Known for serving fast, affordable, and tasty coffee and beverages, it’s a good stop for a quick caffeine boost, casual hangout, or laid-back drink while exploring the city.

Key Highlights:
- Local Cambodian Coffee Chain: Tube Coffee was established in 2017 by Cambodian coffee lovers and has rapidly expanded across the country, including into Siem Reap. It focuses on quality drinks with reasonable prices tailored to local tastes.
- Tube Cafe
- Fast & Friendly Service: Designed for quick service — great for take-away coffee, refreshing iced drinks, or a short sit-down break while strolling through town.
- Tube Cafe
- Affordable Menu: Offers a variety of drinks such as espresso, lattes, cappuccinos, iced coffee, tea, smoothies, and light snacks — all with budget-friendly pricing typical of Cambodian chains.
- Tube Cafe
- Casual & Comfortable: Relaxed seating inside or nearby outdoor areas allows you to enjoy your drink quietly or casually meet friends. Customer reviews note that the café vibe is pleasant and welcoming.

Visitor Information:
- Location: 62 Sivutha Blvd, Krong Siem Reap, Cambodia — in a central area that’s easy to reach by walking, tuk-tuk, or bicycle from major hotel zones and the Old Market area. https://maps.app.goo.gl/v7HhbmmwkTSpx5uE8
- Getting There: Quick and easy by foot if you’re already in town center; tuk-tuks and bicycles are also common ways to reach the café.
- Best Time to Visit: Morning to late afternoon (≈6:30 AM – 8:00 PM) — ideal for coffee, iced drinks, or an afternoon break.
- Opening Hours: Typically 6:30 AM – 8:00 PM (daily) — great for breakfast coffee or an after-lunch latte.
- Entrance Fee: Free (pay only for your order).
- Facilities: Casual seating, take-away options, and a friendly barista team.

Tips:
- Great choice for a quick pick-me-up coffee between exploring markets or temples.
- Try an iced coffee or flavored latte especially in hot weather — locals and visitors alike enjoy refreshing drinks here.
- Combine your coffee stop with a short walk around Sivutha Boulevard where many shops, restaurants, and cafés line the street.

Why Visit:
- A homegrown Cambodian coffee brand that’s affordable, approachable, and convenient.
- Tube Cafe
- Handy for a casual coffee break in a central part of Siem Reap.
- Good for take-away or short sit-downs without formal café formality.
