
The Bear Cafe & Pub is a café, restaurant, and pub in Siem Reap that blends relaxed café vibes with casual dining and evening drinks. It’s a popular local spot for breakfast, lunch, dinner, coffee, and beers — a versatile place to relax, eat, or socialize during the day and into the night.

Key Highlights:
- Café & Pub Dining: Serves a mix of Asian, Cambodian, and Western food, from hearty local dishes to salads and snacks, plus a range of drinks including coffee and beer.
- Tripadvisor
- All-Day Menu: Open from early morning (around 7 AM) through the evening (many reviews note service up to around 11 PM), so you can visit for breakfast, lunch, dinner, coffee breaks, or night drinks.
- Tripadvisor
- Comfortable & Versatile Atmosphere: Spacious indoor and outdoor seating with a relaxed vibe — good for families, friends, or solo visitors.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Friendly Service: Visitors frequently mention helpful, polite staff attentive to guests.
- Tripadvisor
- Good Wi-Fi & Work-Friendly: Popular spot for remote work or leisurely browsing with reliable Wi-Fi and air-conditioned comfort.

Visitor Information:
- Location: Preah Sihanouk Ave, Krong Siem Reap (central and accessible by tuk-tuk or walking from many hotel areas). https://maps.app.goo.gl/ovvBCBEcfkreQEws6
- Tripadvisor
- Getting There: Easy to reach by tuk-tuk, bicycle, or walking from major parts of Siem Reap.
- Tripadvisor
- Best Time to Visit: Morning to evening — morning for coffee and breakfast; afternoon for lunch or work; evening for dinner and drinks.
- Tripadvisor
- Opening Hours: Typically 7:00 AM – ~11:00 PM on many days.
- Tripadvisor
- Entrance Fee: Free — pay only for orders.
- Facilities: Free Wi-Fi, indoor/outdoor seating, table service, credit card acceptance, and parking available.
