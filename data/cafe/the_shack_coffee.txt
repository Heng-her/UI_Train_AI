
The Shack Coffee & Tea is a popular casual coffee shop in Siem Reap that’s ideal for sipping quality drinks, relaxing with friends, or taking a refreshing break while exploring the city. With a strong local reputation and a diverse beverage menu, it’s a nice mix of café culture and laid-back hangout — perfect for both short stops and longer chill sessions.

Key Highlights:
- Comfortable Café Vibe: A relaxed setting where visitors can enjoy coffee, tea, fresh juices, smoothies, and other chilled drinks in a casual café environment.
- Diverse Drink Menu: Offers a range of coffee drinks (Americano, espresso, iced coffee, cappuccino), tea options (Ceylon breakfast tea, jasmine tea, herbal infusions), fresh juices, smoothies, mocktails, and milkshakes — great for any time of day.

- Fresh & Healthy Options: Many fruit-based drinks and smoothies make this a great stop in Siem Reap’s warm climate.

- Affordable Prices: Reasonable menu prices for coffee and drinks, making it a popular choice among locals, travelers, and digital nomads alike.
- Friendly Local Spot: Highly rated by visitors for its welcoming atmosphere and good service.

Visitor Information:
- Location: Street 63, Krong Siem Reap 17252, Cambodia — a convenient spot easily reached from the Old Market, Riverside, or Pub Street areas. https://maps.app.goo.gl/9TDWrntq8yN5S6Lv8
- Getting There: Walking, tuk-tuk, or bicycle from most central Siem Reap locations.
- Best Time to Visit: Morning to early evening — perfect for coffee before exploring temples or a cool drink in the afternoon.
- Opening Hours: ~7:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily (check local variations).
- Entrance Fee: Free — pay only for what you order.
- Facilities: Seating inside (and possibly shaded outdoor seating), free Wi-Fi at many times, and a casual relaxed space.
- Accessibility: Easy access and suitable for short visits or longer coffee breaks.
