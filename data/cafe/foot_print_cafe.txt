
Footprint Cafes is a social-enterprise café and community hub in Siem Reap that blends great food and beverages with a mission to support local communities and sustainable development. It’s a relaxing spot to enjoy coffee, meals, and refreshing drinks while also contributing to a positive impact in the region.

Key Highlights:
- Social Enterprise Mission: Footprint Cafes operates under a “People, Planet, Profit” model — meaning it provides excellent employment and training for local staff, sources ethically and locally, and donates 100% of its net profits back to community education and entrepreneurial grants.
- Footprint Cafes
- +1
- Community Empowerment: Since opening its first café in November 2016, it has supported local projects like libraries, educational programs, and community centers in the Siem Reap region through grant funding.
- Footprint Cafes
- Book-Lined, Relaxed Space: The café features hundreds of books to browse or purchase, with a calm, library-like indoor area and a pleasant outdoor seating space, perfect for reading, relaxing, or catching up with friends.
- Tripadvisor
- Diverse Menu: Serves a range of Cambodian and international dishes — including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options — alongside freshly brewed coffee, teas, smoothies, juices, and even cocktails.
- Magical Cafés
- Friendly Baristas & Quality Coffee: Known for one of the best coffee roasts in town with welcoming service from well-trained staff.

Visitor Information:
- Location: Street 26, about 50 meters from the Siem Reap River, in a quieter part of town but still walkable from central markets and the riverfront area. https://maps.app.goo.gl/3SjpbeJzm3feN7PAA
- Footprint Cafes
- Getting There: Easily reached by walking, tuk-tuk, bicycle, or motorbike from most of Siem Reap’s tourist and hotel areas.
- Footprint Cafes
- Best Time to Visit: Morning to early evening (≈07:00 AM to 07:00 PM) — great for coffee breaks, brunch, lunch, or a relaxing early dinner.
- Tripadvisor
- Opening Hours: Daily around 07:00 – 19:00 (may vary slightly).
- Tripadvisor
- Entrance Fee: Free — you only pay for your orders.
- Facilities: Free Wi-Fi, outdoor seating, indoor lounging among books, vegetarian/vegan options, parking, and wheelchair accessible areas.
