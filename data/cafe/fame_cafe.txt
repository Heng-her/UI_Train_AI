
FAME Café in Siem Reap is a popular local café brand with multiple locations around the city, known for serving great coffee, refreshing drinks, and a welcoming atmosphere at very affordable prices. It’s a go-to spot for both locals and visitors who want a comfortable place to relax, work, or meet friends with good coffee and tea in hand.

Key Highlights:
- Local Favorite Café Brand: A set of charming cafés throughout Siem Reap offering quality coffee, teas, frappes, and light snacks at prices typically around $1.50–$2.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Great Coffee Variety: Enjoy classics like Americano, condensed milk coffee, iced lattes, and matcha drinks, plus smoothies and cold beverages — tasty options for any time of day.
- Foodpanda
- Friendly Service & Comfortable Space: Visitors appreciate the kind staff, air-conditioned seating, free Wi-Fi, and relaxed vibe — ideal for chilling or working.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Indoor & Outdoor Seating: Many branches offer both indoor comfort and outdoor seating, so you can relax how you like.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Free Extras: Some locations include complimentary green tea with orders — a refreshing touch in hot Cambodian weather.
- Laptop Friendly Cafe
- Accessible & Affordable: A great budget-friendly choice compared to touristy cafés, with generous seating and often power outlets for laptops.
