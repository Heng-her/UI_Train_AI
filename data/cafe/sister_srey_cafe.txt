
Sister Srey Cafe is a charming social-enterprise café in Siem Reap that combines quality coffee, wholesome food, and a community-focused mission. It’s a relaxed and friendly spot popular with tourists and locals alike — perfect for breakfast, lunch, coffee breaks, and a calm riverside stop in the heart of town.

Key Highlights:
- Social Enterprise Mission: Founded in 2012 by two Australian sisters, Sister Srey Cafe aims to support and empower Khmer youth by providing hospitality training, fair wages, and growth opportunities to local students. Profits help fund community programs and support ongoing Cambodian initiatives like landmine-clearance efforts.

- Relaxed Riverside Atmosphere: Situated near the Siem Reap River and the Old Market area, this café offers a peaceful, open-air vibe with outdoor seating, wooden tables, and a laid-back feel — ideal for unwinding after exploring the city.

- Diverse & Healthy Menu: Sister Srey serves breakfast, brunch, and lunch with a mix of Khmer and Western dishes — including smoothie bowls, veggie burgers, sandwiches, healthy bowls, and fresh juices. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are available.
