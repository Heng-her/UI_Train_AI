
CAFISTA Coffee is a popular café and casual eatery in Siem Reap that’s beloved for its creative coffee drinks, relaxed atmosphere, and affordable food and beverage menu. It’s a favorite stop for travelers looking for a cozy spot to enjoy a quality cup of coffee, catch up on work, or grab a tasty meal without breaking the budget.

Key Highlights:
- Creative & Varied Drinks: Offers a wide range of beverages, from classic espresso, cappuccino, and lattes to imaginative options like Tiramisu Cream Latte, Mojito Coffee, Black Pink, and Matcha Coconut. You’ll also find non-coffee choices like fresh teas, lemonade, and fruit-based sodas.
- Foodpanda
- Affordable Food Menu: In addition to drinks, CAFISTA serves casual meals such as fried rice, pasta dishes (e.g., Spaghetti Hot Basil, Truffle Pasta), sandwiches, salads, and desserts like cheesecake and cakes — all at very reasonable prices.

- Cozy & Friendly Ambiance: Visitors often highlight the warm, welcoming staff and pleasant interior, which makes it great for casual meetups, relaxed coffee breaks, or even remote working with Wi-Fi.
- Wanderlog
- Work-Friendly Space: The café doubles as a comfortable workspace with reliable internet, making it a favorite among students, digital nomads, and people on longer stays.

- Photo-Worthy Vibes: The café’s quirky elements — like decorative details and creative drink names — also make it a fun spot to capture café photos.
