
The Little Red Fox Espresso Cafe is one of Siem Reap’s most beloved specialty coffee spots, tucked into the trendy Kandal Village area. Founded by Australian baristas and run with a blend of Cambodian hospitality and creative café culture, it’s a favorite for visitors and locals alike who want excellent coffee, tasty brunch food, and a relaxed café atmosphere.

Key Highlights:
- Specialty Coffee Focus: Known for well‑crafted espresso drinks, flat whites, cold brews, unique lattes (e.g., lemongrass or ginger), and classic cappuccinos — many visitors describe the coffee as among the best in Siem Reap.
- Cambodia is Happiness

- Laid‑Back, Stylish Setting: The café has a minimalist yet cozy vibe with eclectic décor, greenery, and both indoor and outdoor seating — including a pleasant balcony area perfect for sipping a coffee or working on your laptop.

- Breakfast & Brunch Menu: Beyond coffee, the café serves breakfast and brunch items such as bagels, smashed avocado on sourdough, chia muesli, tempeh toast, pancakes, and baked goods — many of which are vegetarian‑friendly or vegan options.

- Friendly, Welcoming Service: Guests often highlight the warm, attentive staff, creating a relaxed, community‑oriented atmosphere.
- Wanderlog
- Sustainability & Local Culture: The café embraces sustainable practices and showcases local art and music, giving you a taste of Siem Reap’s creative community.
