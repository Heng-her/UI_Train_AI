
The Bean Embassy Roastery & Specialty Coffee is one of Siem Reap’s top specialty coffee destinations, beloved by locals, expatriates, and travelers who appreciate high-quality coffee, cozy vibes, and a laid-back café atmosphere. It blends expertly roasted coffee with delicious food, relaxed indoor/outdoor spaces, and a creative café culture.

Key Highlights:
- Specialty Coffee & In-House Roastery: Focuses on farm-to-cup coffee experiences, sourcing quality beans (including local and international origins) and roasting them on site for rich, fresh flavors.
- The Bean Embassy
- Expert Baristas: Drinks are crafted by skilled baristas — some with national competition experience — ensuring each cup is brewed with precision and depth of flavor.
