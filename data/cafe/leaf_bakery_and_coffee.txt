
Leaf Bakery and Coffee is a cozy, highly-rated café in Siem Reap that’s quickly become a favorite for travelers and locals looking for great coffee, tasty baked goods, and a relaxed atmosphere away from the busiest streets.

Key Highlights:
- Charming & Relaxed Vibe: A small, simple café with a calming indoor space — a perfect escape from the heat or a busy day of sightseeing.
- Wanderlog
- Delicious Coffee & Specialty Drinks: Known especially for its hand-whisked matcha latte and quality brewed coffee, both at very reasonable prices.
- Wanderlog
- Bakery & Baked Treats: Offers freshly made baked goods like banana muffins, cheesecakes, cookies, and brownies that pair beautifully with coffee.
- Wanderlog
- Vegan-Friendly Options: Includes vegan milk choices (like oat milk) and some plant-based menu items, making it a great stop for diverse dietary preferences.
- Wanderlog
- Friendly Service: Reviewers note welcoming and attentive staff who may even offer complimentary touches like cold water or treats.
