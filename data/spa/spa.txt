
Old Market (Phsar Chas) is one of the oldest and most authentic local markets in Siem Reap, located near the river and close to Pub Street. It has long been a central trading area for local residents and remains a vibrant place to experience everyday Cambodian life. The market blends traditional Khmer culture with tourism, offering everything from fresh produce and spices to souvenirs and street food.


Key Highlights:
- Traditional Local Market: A true glimpse into daily Cambodian life, with vendors selling fresh meat, fish, vegetables, and herbs.
- Souvenirs & Handicrafts: Silk scarves, handmade crafts, wood carvings, paintings, and souvenirs for visitors.
- Street Food Area: Local snacks, fresh fruit, grilled meats, noodles, and Khmer desserts at affordable prices.
- Cultural Atmosphere: Bustling, colorful, and lively—especially in the morning and late afternoon.
- Local Shopping Experience: Prices are generally lower than tourist shops, and bargaining is common.

Visitor Information:
- Location: Central Siem Reap, near Pub Street and the Siem Reap River. https://maps.app.goo.gl/CqKzTv2d7spWuDo29
- Getting There: Easily accessible by walking, tuk-tuk, or bicycle.
- Best Time to Visit: Early morning (6:00–9:00 AM) for local market life, or late afternoon for shopping and food.
- Opening Hours: Daily, approximately 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM (some stalls stay open later).
- Entrance Fee: Free.
- Facilities: Food stalls, small eateries, nearby cafés, ATMs, and restrooms nearby.
- Accessibility: Narrow aisles and uneven floors; can be crowded during peak hours.

Tips:
- Bring small cash (Cambodian riel or USD).
- Bargaining is expected but should be polite and friendly.
- Watch personal belongings in crowded areas.
- Try local snacks like num banh chok (Khmer noodles) or fresh coconut.
- Visit early for a more authentic local experience.

Why Visit:
- Experience authentic Cambodian daily life.
- Shop for local products and souvenirs at reasonable prices.
- Taste traditional Khmer street food.
- Enjoy the contrast between local culture and nearby nightlife areas.
- A must-see spot for cultural exploration in Siem Reap.
