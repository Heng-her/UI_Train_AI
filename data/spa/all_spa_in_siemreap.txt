Perfect for rejuvenating after exploring the temples of Angkor or wandering the lively Old Market area. Many combine friendly service with serene settings to soothe both body and mind.

Tips:
- Book treatments in advance, especially during peak travel seasons.
- Wear light, comfortable clothing to and from your appointment.
- Communicate your pressure preference early (e.g., light, medium, deep).
- Many spas are open late into the evening — ideal after a long day of sightseeing.
- Tipping (5–10%) is appreciated for excellent service.

Why Visit:
- Relax tired muscles after days exploring Angkor Wat and surrounding temples.
- Experience traditional Khmer massage and healing techniques.
- Enjoy a peaceful escape from the heat and bustle of central Siem Reap.
- Complement spa time with other wellness experiences like aromatherapy and reflexology.
