
Devatara Spa Siem Reap is a large spa on Taphul Road offering a wide variety of treatments for visitors seeking a full wellness experience.

Key Highlights:
- Body treatments, facials, and scrubs
- Spacious facility with multiple rooms
- Professional staff
- Convenient central location

Visitor Information:
- Taphul Road, Siem Reap, http://www.devataraspa.com/
- , https://maps.google.com/?q=Devatara+Spa+Siem+Reap

Tips:
- Book longer sessions to save money
- Arrive early for check-in and relaxation
- Bring cash for tips
- Wear light clothing
- Ask for popular signature treatments

Why Visit:
- Ideal for a full day spa experience
- Variety of wellness and massage options
- Spacious and comfortable setting
- Professional and friendly staff
