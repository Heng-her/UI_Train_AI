
Blue Spa Siem Reap is a well-liked spa on Sok San Road offering excellent service and a variety of treatments for visitors seeking relaxation.

Key Highlights:
- Thai massage and reflexology
- Body scrubs and facials
- Friendly staff and clean facilities
- Central location on Sok San Road

Visitor Information:
- Sok San Road, Siem Reap, https://www.bluespasiemreap.com https://maps.google.com/?q=Blue+Spa+Siem+Reap

Tips:
- Check prices before booking
- Book during off-peak hours for quieter experience
- Bring cash
- Ask about combo packages
- Arrive 10–15 minutes early

Why Visit:
- Popular spa with excellent service
- Variety of treatments available
- Central and convenient location
- Friendly, professional staff
