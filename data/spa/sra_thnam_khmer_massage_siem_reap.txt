
Sra Thnam is a local Khmer massage spa offering authentic techniques in a welcoming environment, popular for traditional Cambodian treatments.

Key Highlights:
- Authentic Khmer massage
- Foot reflexology and body wellness services
- Friendly and professional therapists
- Highly rated by visitors

Visitor Information:

- , https://maps.google.com/?q=Sra+Thnam+Khmer+Massage+Siem+Reap

Tips:
- Wear casual clothes
- Bring cash
- Book ahead during busy days
- Hydrate after massage
- Ask for pressure level preference

Why Visit:
- Authentic Cambodian massage
- Great local experience
- Friendly and skilled staff
- Affordable compared to luxury spas
