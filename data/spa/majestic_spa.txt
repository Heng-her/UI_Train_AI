
Majestic Spa is a stylish spa on Sivutha Boulevard, offering a variety of massage and wellness treatments in a comfortable, professional environment.

Key Highlights:
- Foot massage and body scrub
- Relaxing and modern interiors
- Professional and friendly staff
- Central location for tourists
