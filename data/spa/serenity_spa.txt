
Serenity Spa is a highly reviewed massage spa near the Old Market area, known for its calming atmosphere and professional therapists.

Key Highlights:
- Traditional Khmer massage
- Aromatherapy and body treatments
- Convenient central location
- Clean and tranquil environment

Visitor Information:
- Sivutha Road, Near Old Market, Siem Reap, https://www.serenityspa.asia https://maps.google.com/?q=Serenity+Spa+Siem+Reap

Tips:
- Arrive early at peak times
- Book ahead to secure your slot
- Wear light, comfortable clothing
- Bring water for hydration after treatments
- Ask for traditional Khmer massage for authenticity

Why Visit:
- Convenient location near Old Market
- Authentic Cambodian massage experience
- Relaxing environment for tourists
- Highly rated service and friendly staff
