
Siem Reap is home to a wide range of spas offering traditional Khmer massage, wellness treatments, and modern relaxation services. These spas are conveniently located throughout the city, making them ideal for tourists seeking rest after temple visits and sightseeing.

Key Highlights:
- Traditional Khmer and aromatherapy massage
- Central locations near Old Market, Pub Street, and Sok San Road
- Options ranging from luxury spas to affordable massage centers
- Professional therapists and relaxing environments

Tips:
- Book spa appointments in advance during peak tourist season
- Arrive early to relax before treatments
- Bring cash as some spas may not accept cards
- Stay hydrated after massage treatments
- Ask about traditional Khmer massage options

Why Visit:
- Relax after visiting Angkor temples
- Experience authentic Cambodian wellness traditions
- Wide range of spa options for every budget
- Convenient locations throughout Siem Reap
