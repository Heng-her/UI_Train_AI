
Culinary and food tours in Siem Reap combine local market visits, Khmer cooking classes, street food adventures, and tasting experiences. These group tours take travelers into the heart of Cambodian cuisine and culture through hands‑on activities and authentic local food experiences. 
Canby Publications

Key Highlights:
- Learn to cook traditional Khmer dishes with local chefs
- Journey Cambodia
- Visit busy markets and street food spots
- Canby Publications
- Sample classic Cambodian snacks and meals
- Shared food experiences with fellow travelers

Tips:
- Wear comfortable clothing for market walks.
- Bring a small bag for market purchases.
- Book classes early as they fill up.
- Ask if vegetarian options are available.
- Try local drinks and snacks.

Why Visit:
- Learn authentic Khmer cooking techniques.
- Taste a wide range of Cambodian flavours.
- Visit local lifestyle hubs like markets.
- Shared tours make new food friends.
