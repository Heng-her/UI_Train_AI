
Floating village group tours in Siem Reap offer shared boat and cultural experiences exploring Cambodia’s unique Tonlé Sap Lake communities, including stilted houses, mangrove channels, and daily life on the water. These tours combine scenic cruising with insights into traditional lake ecology and village life, perfect for those seeking a cultural and natural adventure beyond the temples.
My Siem Reap Tours

Key Highlights:
- Visit iconic floating villages such as Kampong Phluk and Chong Khneas with local boat guides.
- My Siem Reap Tours
- Experience traditional lake life and see stilted homes, floating schools, and mangrove waterways.
- My Siem Reap Tours
- Perfect for sunrise or afternoon cruises with calm water views and photo opportunities.
- My Siem Reap Tours
- Shared group tours are both social and affordable for travelers on a budget.

Tips:
- Aim for early morning or late afternoon departures for cooler weather and calmer waters.
- My Siem Reap Tours
- Bring insect repellent, sunscreen, and a hat — lake tours can get exposed and sunny.
- Check the season — water levels change significantly between wet and dry seasons.
- Respect local culture; ask before photographing people living in the villages.
- Wear comfortable shoes since some tours may include short walks or village stops.
