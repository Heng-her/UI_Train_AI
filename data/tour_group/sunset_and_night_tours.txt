
Sunset & night group tours in Siem Reap give travelers opportunities to see Angkor’s iconic sights at magical lighting, enjoy evening boat cruises on Tonlé Sap Lake, explore night markets, and experience local culture after dark. These tours blend scenic views with local life and entertainment in a shared group setting. 
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siem_Reap

Key Highlights:
- Watch spectacular sunset views over temple silhouettes or on Tonlé Sap Lake
- Evening boat rides with peaceful lake panoramas
- Visit the bustling Angkor Night Market after sunset
- Wikipedia
- Cultural experiences such as Phare Cambodian Circus (evening show)
- Mango Tiger DMC

Tips:
- Bring a light jacket; evenings can be cooler.
- Arrive at the night market early to avoid crowds.
- Confirm sunset cruise departure times.
- Wear comfortable shoes for night walks.
- Bring bug repellent near water areas.

Why Visit:
- Enjoy the magical transition from day to night over Angkor temples.
- Experience local cuisine and nightlife.
- Unique views of Siem Reap from the water.
- Great for evening photography.
