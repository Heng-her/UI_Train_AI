
Kulen Mountain and waterfall group tours lead travelers into the sacred Phnom Kulen National Park to see dramatic waterfalls, ancient carvings, reclining Buddha statues, and rural villages. These tours mix nature, spirituality, and cultural insights in a scenic mountain setting.

Tips:
- Wear water‑friendly shoes for waterfall visits.
- Bring a change of clothes for swimming at falls.
- Pack insect repellent for jungle trails.
- Ask about lunch breaks and packed lunch options.
- Start early to avoid midday heat.

Why Visit:
- Escape the city for nature and cool waterfalls.
- Learn about local traditions and spiritual practices.
- Enjoy scenic views and photo ops.
- Great for travelers seeking nature + culture.
