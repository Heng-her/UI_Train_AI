
Angkor Temple group tours offer an organized way to explore Cambodia’s most famous archaeological sites, including Angkor Wat, Bayon, and Ta Prohm. These tours often include sunrise or full-day itineraries, giving cultural context and history with expert guides.

Key Highlights:
- Explore iconic temples with licensed guides (Journey Cambodia)
- Sunrise or sunset views at Angkor Wat and Angkor Thom (Siem Reap Shuttle Tours)
- Learn about Khmer architecture, history, and religion (Discova)
- Small-group tours for social interaction and photography opportunities

Tips:
- Arrive early for sunrise tours to avoid crowds
- Wear lightweight, modest clothing covering shoulders and knees
- Bring sunscreen, hats, and water
- Confirm hotel pickup times with your operator
- Prepare for long walking distances at temple sites

Why Visit:
- Experience world-famous Angkor Wat at sunrise
- Gain insight into Khmer history and culture
- Capture iconic temple photography
- Enjoy structured small-group travel with knowledgeable guides
