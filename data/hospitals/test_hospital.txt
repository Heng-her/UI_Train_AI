
777 Siem Reap Polyclinic (Muntipet 777 Siem Reap) is a local private healthcare facility located directly on National Road 6 in the Svay Dangkum district. As a "Muntipet" (Polyclinic), it serves primarily the local Cambodian population and travelers passing through the main highway. It is a standard Cambodian-style clinic that provides general medical services, inpatient observation, and basic emergency care. While it lacks the advanced technology and English-speaking environment of international hospitals, it offers a convenient and affordable option for simple medical needs, particularly for those staying in hotels or guesthouses along the National Road 6 strip.

Key Highlights:
- Prime Highway Location: Situated right on National Road 6 (the main airport road), it is highly visible and accessible for anyone staying at the large resorts and hotels that line this major avenue.
- Affordable Local Pricing: As a facility targeting local residents, the fee structure is significantly lower than tourist-centric hospitals, making it a budget-friendly choice for minor ailments.
- General Medicine & IV Therapy: The clinic specializes in treating common tropical symptoms like high fever, dehydration, and fatigue, often utilizing IV vitamin drips which are a popular local treatment.
- Inpatient Observation: Equipped with patient beds for overnight stays, allowing for monitoring of conditions like Dengue fever or severe food poisoning without the cost of a luxury hospital room.
- 24/7 Availability: Like most comprehensive polyclinics in the city, it generally operates round-the-clock to handle sudden illnesses or minor traffic accidents that occur on the busy highway.

Visitor Information:
- Location: No. 777, National Road 6, Svay Dangkum, Siem Reap. (Located on the main highway, typically accessible from the city center heading towards the airport).
- Getting There: It is very easy to find; simply tell your driver "Muntipet 777 on National Road 6." It is about a 10-minute tuk-tuk ride from the city center.
- Best Time to Visit: Mid-morning or early afternoon is best for general consultations when senior doctors are most likely to be present.
- Opening Hours:
- General/Emergency: Generally Open 24 Hours.
- Consultation Fee: Approx. $10–$20 USD for an initial visit (Local pricing).
- Facilities: Basic inpatient rooms (fan or AC), on-site pharmacy, and general treatment rooms.
- Accessibility: Ground floor access directly from the highway, making it wheelchair accessible.

Tips:
- Language Barrier: Expect limited English proficiency among the nursing staff. A translation app or a Khmer-speaking guide is highly recommended.
- Cash Transactions: Credit cards are likely not accepted. Bring US Dollars or Riel cash.
- Manage Expectations: This is a functional local clinic, not a resort hospital. Facilities will be clean but basic.
- Traffic Caution: Be careful when entering or exiting the clinic, as National Road 6 is a high-speed, busy highway.
- Use for Minor Issues: Ideal for getting a quick prescription, wound dressing, or IV fluids. For major surgery or complex diagnostics, refer to a CPA3 hospital (Provincial) or an International facility

Why Visit:
- Convenient access if your hotel is on National Road 6.
- Very affordable rates for basic medical care and medicine.
- Quick attention for common travel sicknesses like dehydration.
- Avoid the congestion of the downtown area.
- Experience a typical Cambodian medical setting.
